Developer note - Unofficial community mods

The mods and links in this thread are shared by their creator. We don’t review or test these files and can’t verify their safety or compatibility. Any Discord server linked here is run independently from the studio.

Back up your saves before installing any mods. Mods can corrupt your save files or prevent the game from launching, even if they worked before a game update. We can’t recover corrupted saves or fix issues caused by mods.

**Bills** puts **MyBills** on the phone and on the company computer. It lists the company bills. Paying uses the company account. What the account cannot cover stays open. It is a PineLoader mod for **Pionero Capital**.

**1.0.3** needs **PineLoader 1.1.0-alpha**.

Phone and computer show the same open list. The pay control names the amount. Phone and Computer are not switches. The apps stay while the mod is on. A debit that did not close the bill is not taken again after a restart.

**Archive** is off until `Config\Bills.ini` says `Archive=true`. It lists bills this mod has paid. Those bills stay in `PineLoader\Mods\Bills\bills.json`.

**AutoPay** is experimental and off. It stays off unless that ini says `AutoPay=true`.

**Install**
Install PineLoader first and close the game. Copy the `Bills` folder into `PineLoader\Mods`. It needs `Bills.dll` and `bills-agent.jar`.
