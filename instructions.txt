Bills 1.0.1
A PineLoader mod for Pionero Capital.
MyBills lists the company bills on the phone and on the company computer.
A bill is paid from the company account. What the account cannot cover stays open.

Install

Install PineLoader first. Then install Bills.

1. PineLoader

   Download PineLoader 1.0.3:
   https://github.com/EuroCent82/PineLoader/releases/latest

   The file is PineLoader-1.0.3-win-x64.zip.
   Unpack it into a folder named PineLoader next to the game.
   For Pionero Capital the program then sits here:

   Pionero Capital\PineLoader\PineLoader.exe

   Close the game. Start PineLoader.exe once.
   The loader saves the official game file as
   PioneroCapital-Win64-Game.exe
   and points PioneroCapital-Win64-Shipping.exe at itself.
   Leave the Steam launch options empty.
   Play in Steam starts PineLoader first.

   The same guide is on the site, under Documentation.

2. Bills

   Download Bills 1.0.1.
   The file is Bills-1.0.1.zip.
   Copy the Bills folder from that zip into:

   Pionero Capital\PineLoader\Mods\

   You then have these two files:

   PineLoader\Mods\Bills\Bills.dll
   PineLoader\Mods\Bills\bills-agent.jar

   Start the game through PineLoader, or press Play in Steam.
   The loader writes Config\Bills.ini when that file is missing.

   1.0.1 loads the phone page and the company computer app.
   In 1.0.0 the phone icon was there, but the page stayed empty
   and the computer app was missing.

Phone and computer

On the phone the app is named MyBills.
Inside the app, the title follows the language selected in the game.
On the company computer, MyBills opens full screen, with the tabs at the top.
Open is the tab that is showing when the app starts.

Open lists the company bills that are still unpaid.
Choosing a row pays that bill from the company account.
If the account cannot cover it, the bill stays open.

Archive

Archive is off until Config\Bills.ini says Archive=true.
The second tab then lists bills this mod has paid.
A payment made somewhere else is not added to that list.
The remembered bills are in PineLoader\Mods\Bills\bills.json.
Leave that file in place if you want to keep them.

AutoPay

AutoPay is experimental and off.
When it is on, a due bill can be paid from the company account.
What the account cannot cover stays open.

Enabled=false turns MyBills off.
The remembered file stays where it is.
